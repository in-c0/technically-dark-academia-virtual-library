# TDA — Player-Facing Content Pack v0.1
**Technically Dark Academia** · **Technology IS magic.**

Creative copy for the *virtual-first* TDA game. **Draft, not approved Steam store copy.** Only advertise features after they are implemented and verified. The initial product is a fictional online bookstore; there is no real-time physical bookstore integration or verified partner store.

## Brand voice
Warm, observant, quietly curious. Thoughtful without intellectual gatekeeping. A hint of wonder without relentless mystery. Never demand productivity or punish the choice to just wander. Use very short prompts in the interface; let the space and books carry the feeling.

**Core line:** *A bookshop you can wander into, from anywhere.*
**Secondary line:** *Come for a book. Stay for everything it reminds you of.*
**Invitation:** *You don't need to know what you're looking for.*

## Draft Steam short description (EN)
Step inside an atmospheric virtual bookshop. Wander among ideas, discover books, make visual journals, and spend quiet time alongside friends. Your next favorite thing might be something you weren't looking for.

## Draft Steam About This Game (EN)
Some of the best things you find in a bookshop aren't the things you came to buy.

TDA is a place for slow exploration. Follow a book cover that catches your eye. Wander into a subject you know nothing about. Keep a thought in your notebook, or share a corner of the room with someone who loves reading just as much as you do.

Browse shelves that invite discovery, open book information and available previews, and collect the ideas you want to return to. Give your interests a home in a personal visual journal. Choose an atmosphere for browsing, studying, researching, or simply being alone.

There is no right way to spend an evening here. You can come with a purpose, or none at all.

*Technology IS magic.*

## Opening moment: first 90 seconds
1. **Outside, after rain.** The window glows; a faint sound of turning pages and distant conversation. No forced tutorial panel.
2. **Threshold.** A discreet card reads: “You don't need to know what you're looking for.” Action: **Step inside**.
3. **Books before menus.** The player's gaze lands on a human-scale discovery table with distinct visual subjects. Optional prompts: **See what's new**, **Wander**, **Find a subject**.
4. **A book in hand.** Opening its card reveals provenance, description, access-appropriate preview action, **Keep for later**, and **Make a note**.
5. **A place to keep things.** The first saved item appears in a notebook and can be returned to without leaving the present shelf. No requirement to publish, register friends, or complete a quest.

## Three ORIGINAL sample journals, not real published books
The following entries are invented TDA demo content, **not** metadata for an existing work.

### “An Atlas of Small Weather”
*Field Notes · Entry 01: Before the Rain*
The leaves gave it away before the sky did. A quieter green beneath the silver one, a pause in the birdsong, the faint metallic smell rising from the pavement. I had walked this road a hundred times without seeing any of it. Today I stopped long enough to notice. Perhaps a field guide begins there: not with an answer, but with a better question.

### “Catalogue of Unfinished Machines”
*Mechanisms · Entry 07: The Clock That Forgot an Hour*
The first version moved beautifully and told the wrong time. The second told the right time and barely moved at all. I kept both sketches. Somewhere between them is the machine I wanted to make—and the reason I wanted to make it. A useful notebook remembers what worked. A beloved notebook also remembers what almost worked.

### “A Shelf of Other Lives”
*Other Lives · Entry 03: On Unexpected Books*
I came looking for a book about the stars and left thinking about a gardener I have never met. Perhaps that is what I love about bookshops: strangers have already arranged a thousand doors for us, and no one insists which one we should open first.
